How we engage

One contract. A written scope. A clear finish line.

Every engagement is with Empirical Co Pty Ltd and priced per project. No rate cards and no open-ended hours.

Who you contract
Empirical Co Pty Ltd. Ongoing clients sign a master services agreement, with a statement of work for each project.
What we agree
Deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria and timing, in writing, before work starts.
How it’s priced
A project fee, milestone payments, or cost-plus production when an event carries significant third-party spend. Quoted for each project.
Who does the work
A principal leads every engagement. When the work needs specialist build, AV, staffing or creative, we engage vetted partners and manage them within our scope.

About

A small firm, built to finish things.

Empirical Co is an Australian company working with brands, agencies and organisations in Australia and across APAC. We take on projects end to end: the brief, the plan, the production and the handover.

The firm is small by design. A principal leads every engagement, and we bring in vetted production and creative partners when the work calls for them. Clients get senior attention and one accountable contract without paying for a large standing team.

The name describes the method. We start from evidence, agree what done looks like, and deliver it. Our mark is a bell curve, the shape you get when you measure carefully.
